# The Orthodox Lens — Version 12

## What is actually included
- 200 distinct movie titles, deduplicated by normalized title + release year, preserving genuinely different remakes.
- 105 original **provisional** movie-review pages: 93 carried over, plus 12 expanded from specifically located official BBFC content-advice pages.
- 95 additional movie-specific **research-pending** pages, with review-checklist, film-source discovery links, and five general post-viewing conversation prompts. These are not complete reviews.
- Scripture references with a review-specific explanatory connection on all 105 provisional reviews.
- A verifiable *paraphrase* (not a direct quote) of St. Basil the Great's *Address to Young Men on the Right Use of Greek Literature*, linked to a public text, on selected reviews where relevant. Patristic quotations are never fabricated.
- Five family discussion questions per provisional review and on all research-pending pages (clearly labelled preliminary prompts).
- Authentic official movie poster lookup through the preexisting Netlify Function + TMDB; artwork is fetched dynamically using TMDB_API_KEY and is not repackaged or generated by AI. Some titles may have no match; styled text cover remains.
- New About page, independence and pastoral statements, Philippians 4:8 homepage theme, retained original layout.
- Explicit content verification and theological-approval statuses. No review was falsely marked approved.

## Limitations / editorial honesty
**This release does not claim to contain 200 fully researched or theologically approved reviews.** The number 200 counts distinct movie entries. All 105 source-linked verdicts are **provisional**, and some older material is sourced incompletely; a full content-warning verification is not certified. The remaining 95 pages are clearly pending research. Final theological approval must come from a human editor. Some prior entries and release years may require additional checking.

## Source policies
- IMDb / Common Sense Media / Dove search links in pending entries are **discovery only**, not verification. Film-specific BBFC links in the new 12 entries support their written content cautions.
- Scripture references are cited by book/chapter/verse; biblical text is not fabricated.
- The St. Basil reference is a linked paraphrase. Do not publish a quotation under his name without checking exact translation and passage.
- TMDB attribution and nonendorsement notice are included. A revenue-generating site requires its own commercial-use licensing review with TMDB.

## Deploying to the connected Netlify site
In the existing GitHub repository, replace the project contents with the files in this ZIP (keep `netlify.toml`, `netlify/functions` and `public` at repository root), then commit to `main` and verify the Netlify production deploy. Do not add the TMDB API key to GitHub. The existing `TMDB_API_KEY` Netlify environment secret should continue to work on production.

## Netlify limitation
The production Netlify build provides poster lookup and static seed reviews. The Editorial studio's AI generation and database-save routes are not yet Netlify-compatible: it requires a persistent database and authenticated serverless implementation. Do not treat the existing static UI as a working production editorial backend.
