# Catalog screening follow-up

Nine titles were checked. Six Editorial Studio drafts were saved and verified at revision 3. Wicked received an incidental 1/5 and retains its Neutral verdict. The five context edits leave their numerical ratings open. Three other findings are recorded here for research.

| Film | Studio status | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wicked (2024) | 1/5 saved | GLAAD identifies Pfannee, one of Glinda’s school friends, as briefly showing attraction to Prince Fiyero when he arrives. The scene is incidental. The digital bonus scene in which Pfannee introduces himself is outside the theatrical cut. [Source 1](https://assets.glaad.org/m/619bf8f01c5e27d7/original/GLAAD-2025-Studio-Responsibility-Index.pdf) |
| Sonic the Hedgehog 2 (2022) | Context saved | Agent Stone makes latte art of himself and Robotnik and appears pleased when Robotnik arrives at the café. [Source 1](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/movie-reviews/sonic-the-hedgehog-2) [Source 2](https://www.scripts.com/script-pdf-body.php?id=26853) |
| Elio (2025) | Context saved | Pixar chief creative officer Pete Docter described removing LGBTQ-related material during development. Reports about those earlier drafts should not be attributed to the released film. | The Little Mermaid (1989) | Context saved | Animator accounts identify drag performer Divine as a visual inspiration for Ursula. Commentary about the director’s other films does not establish a scene in this release. [Source 1](https://gkids.com/films/the-colors-within/) |
| Hamilton (2020) | Research note | This entry covers the filmed Broadway production released in 2020.
