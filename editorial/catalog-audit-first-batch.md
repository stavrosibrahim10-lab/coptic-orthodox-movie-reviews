## Proposed edits

- **Inside Out 2** — Anchor the self-understanding theme in the climax, where Joy allows difficult memories back into Riley’s developing sense of self. Propose 4/5 based on its role in the climax. The parent guide describes Val as a teammate and directly addresses the rumored romance. [Disney/Pixar screenplay](https://assets.debut.disney.com/documents/Inside_Out_2_Script.pdf) and [Plugged In](https://www.pluggedin.com/movie-reviews/inside-out-2-2024/).
- **Luca and Frozen II** — The checked guides describe the relationships in question as friendships. Retain specific interpretive notes where relevant and leave film-wide scores pending. [Luca guide](https://www.pluggedin.com/movie-reviews/luca-2021/) and [Frozen II guide](https://www.pluggedin.com/movie-reviews/frozen-ii-2019/).
- **Strange World** — Propose 10+ under the site’s available age bands because of recurring scenes of peril. Common Sense Media recommends 8+. [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/movie-reviews/strange-world).
- **KPop Demon Hunters** — Retain St. Basil’s text as a paraphrase and identify sections IV–V. The application to film belongs to the reviewer. [St. Basil, translated by Frederick Morgan Padelford](https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/basil_litterature01.htm).
